- L24 (2026-11-08): Method. Each clip is read in 15-second windows every 5 seconds; each reading is corrected with the record's styles and genre (a dance record read below 90 is doubled, 90 to 110 is dropped as a three-quarter misread), and the tempo needs at least two windows, one in six of all windows and a strict majority of the readings. On 38 Deezer previews with a published tempo: 33 right to 1%, 5 with no answer, none wrong (one reading over the whole clip: 34 right, 4 wrong). Sources for the collection: the 30-second previews of Deezer and Apple Music, and the record's own Discogs videos (one per track, matched by title with typo tolerance, skipping videos that name two tracks), downloaded only in part, measured locally and deleted. Where a track had both a video and a preview (28 tracks) they agreed within 2% every time; Florian Meindl's two stored 130s were measured at 130 again. Result: 86 of 115 tracks, stored as approximate lookups (`LOOKUP`), shown with ≈. The other 29: 14 with no video or preview anywhere, 14 whose clips gave no clear beat (broken beats, ambient intros, three-quarter readings), and Boo Williams' "Midnight Express", whose three videos disagree (129.6, 133, 129.6).
